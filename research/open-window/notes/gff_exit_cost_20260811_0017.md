books — kept trades' books are slightly WIDER (mean 14.2 vs 12.5bp;
median 8.7 vs 8.4) because the gate likes crowded mornings. The lift is
signal, not cost-selection, and it survives per-trade measured costs
intact on the same OOS window: BOTH ungated +8.7bp/tr (t 2.44) vs gated
+16.9bp/tr (t 3.51), n 2,614 -> 1,597.

14.7) — the mean sits AT the pre-registration's 20bp stopping threshold,
tail-driven. gff-1 should journal its own exit-time NBBO per trade
(the nosip fill_check pattern) so the stopping rule runs on live numbers,
not this panel's; queued as engineering.
