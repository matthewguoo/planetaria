The wrapper is pure amplification: the option's bp-of-S column can only beat the shares row by luck, because it is the same underlying path minus theta minus a spread that is ~40-100x the share spread as a fraction of capital at risk. A 0DTE makes sense on a signal with edge (it caps risk at the premium and levers a held-for-minutes conviction); it cannot manufacture edge the underlying signal does not have.
