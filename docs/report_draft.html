Planetaria Research · Working Note PR-2026-01

Gating Post-Earnings Announcement Drift with a Large Language Model

A five-year replication with direct training-contamination controls.
Universe after-close earnings events, · model claude-opus-5, effort medium · of inference · drafted 2026-08-06.
Abstract

1Motivation

Post-earnings announcement drift — the tendency of prices to keep moving in the direction of an earnings surprise for weeks after the announcement — is among the oldest and most replicated anomalies in the literature.[1][2][3] Its usual formulation is slow: sort on standardised unexpected earnings, hold for sixty days. The version tested here is the opposite end of the horizon. The release crosses after the close, the tape reprices within the minute, and the question is only whether the first move continues into the next session.

The mechanical form of that trade is simple enough to state in one line: if the after-hours reaction exceeds a threshold, enter in its direction and exit at the next session's close. It is also, on its own, unreliable — it makes money in trending regimes and loses it in stressed ones. The hypothesis under test is that a language model reading the release text can tell those cases apart at announcement time, before the outcome is known: that the release itself contains information about whether the tape's initial verdict will hold.

This is a natural extension of a long line of work showing that the textual content of financial disclosure predicts returns beyond the numbers,[6][7][8] and of more recent findings that large language models extract return-relevant signal from news text.[9] It also inherits that literature's central methodological hazard, which for an LLM is far more acute than for a dictionary method: the model may simply remember what happened.[10][11][12] Most of this note is about that hazard.

Two properties matter for what follows. First, the model never sees the tape reaction it is gating — it reads the release and the pre-announcement run-up, and nothing else. Second, the model never places an order: it returns a schema-constrained verdict, and the agreement gate, the vetoes, and the risk stack decide independently what happens to it.

3Data and universe

Events are 8-K Item 2.02 filings — the SEC form on which US issuers report results — accepted after 16:00 ET, drawn from the thousand most liquid tickers as ranked at the start of each annual window, so no name enters on liquidity it only acquired later. Prices are split- and dividend-adjusted SIP minute and daily bars. The anchor is the announce-day pre-release close, the reaction is the last after-hours print within fifteen minutes of acceptance, and the exit is the next session's 15:55 close.

The distinction the cascade draws is worth stating plainly, because it is the answer to the obvious objection that a strategy scoring three hundred trades a year must be sampling thinly from a much larger pool. It is not. The pool is large — some 1,800 after-close reporters a year in this universe — but the strategy's own 5% gate reduces it to roughly 416, and the watchlist cap to roughly 354. Scoring 312 a year covers 88% of that. The events not scored here are, overwhelmingly, events the production strategy would also never trade.

4Method

4.1 Prompt construction

Each event is one independent API request. There is no conversation history, no shared session, and no cross-event state: every verdict is formed from the system prompt, the task, and one release. Requests declare no tools, so the model cannot retrieve anything. The task text mirrors the production prompt exactly, including its weakest feature — consensus estimates read unknown, because the free data tier the live system runs on keeps no estimate history.

4.2 Contamination controls

The knowledge boundary of claude-opus-5 falls around May 2026. Four of the five years studied are therefore inside the training corpus by construction, and the standard defence — report only the post-cutoff slice[11] — would discard the study. The controls must instead measure the contamination channel. Three are implemented; two have been run.

Table 3 — Contamination controls
ControlMechanismStatus

Memory probe (notext)Ticker and date only, no release. Any edge is pure recall — a direct upper bound.not run

The scrubber for the identity ablation is built and audited: across 1,620 cached releases it removes the ticker, the company name and every year reference on 100% of documents, with a median of 60 substitutions each. The audit is the reason it is trustworthy — an earlier version silently deleted entire documents, and the failure was invisible until the substitution count was checked. Both unrun arms are blocked on inference budget, not on method.

5Results

5.1 The gate separates outcomes

Scoring the raw round trip — enter at the reaction, exit at the next session's close — the gate separates the population sharply. Trades the model agrees with return materially more than the ungated mechanical baseline; trades it vetoes return materially less.

The permutation test shuffles verdict labels within each calendar month, preserving both the monthly mix of directional calls and the monthly return regime, and re-scores the gate a thousand times. The observed separation is not attained in any draw.

The asymmetry across the columns is the substantive finding. The gated column is respectable; the vetoed column is where the money is. In 2022 the mechanical strategy lost 147bp per trade and the gate lifted that to roughly breakeven — not by finding winners, but by declining a set of trades that averaged −501bp. The model is functioning as a tail filter, which is consistent with the same conclusion reached independently on a six-month sample with a different model.

Terminal wealth is not monotone in leverage — volatility decay makes it hump-shaped, so past some exposure the index's own drawdowns compound faster than its gains.[14][15] Solving by bisection returns the search ceiling and a spurious total loss; the figures above come from a scan, which is why some cells report that no exposure reaches the target at all.

5.5 What the brackets cost

Everything to this point measures the raw round trip, because that is what the cached event panel stores. The production strategy does not trade that way. It arms a stop at 2.5× the name's trailing average absolute daily return, floored at 5% and capped at 12%, and a take-profit at twice the stop, either of which the exit enforcer can fire after hours. Resolving all 1,072 gated events against per-event minute bars — stop-first on same-bar touches — gives a materially different answer.

A 5–12% stop is inside the ordinary whipsaw band of a stock that has just moved more than 5% on an earnings release. The stop is not protecting the position from an adverse thesis; it is being triggered by the noise the thesis has to survive. That the same signal produces a Sharpe of 2.71 without brackets and 1.00 with them localises the problem precisely: the gate is picking direction, and the exit policy is giving the direction back.

6Contamination analysis

The evidence available so far is indirect, and points consistently in one direction.

  • Age gradient. Regressing gated per-trade P&L on days since the event gives a slope of per year of age (SE , t = ). A memorised edge should strengthen with corpus age, since older events are more discussed and more repeated in training data. The slope is not significant and its sign is the opposite of the memorisation prediction.
  • Least-contaminated year. 2026 is the year least represented in the corpus and partly beyond the cutoff entirely. It shows the second-highest spread of the six (). Under a recall mechanism it should be the weakest.
  • Permutation null. The observed gate separation is not reached in any of 1,000 within-month verdict shuffles.

None of these is a substitute for the two arms that have not been run. The identity ablation is the strong test — if the edge survives the removal of the issuer's name, it came from reading rather than remembering — and the memory probe bounds the channel directly by asking the model to predict with no release text at all. Until both are run, the correct statement is that the memorisation hypothesis is not supported by the available evidence, not that it has been excluded.

7Limitations

  • Survivorship. The universe is constructed from the current SEC ticker map, so issuers fully delisted since the window are absent. This flatters the 2022 bear regime more than any other period.
  • Execution. Costs are a flat 13bp round trip, not a per-name spread model, and entries assume a fill at the reaction print. The live system currently cannot obtain a fresh after-hours quote at all on its data tier — measured on 2026-08-06, the free feed returned an 8.3-hour-old book with a 10% quoted spread on one name — so the fill assumption is the least-tested part of this note.
  • Consensus. Every prompt reads unknown for estimates. Live has real consensus for roughly two-thirds of names, so this understates the information actually available at decision time.
  • Regime priors. No scrubbing removes what the model knows about the kind of company it is reading. Identity ablation bounds this; it does not eliminate it.
  • Single model, single window. One model family, one five-year span, one market. The 2021–2026 period contains exactly one severe bear market.
  • Paper fills. Nothing here has traded real size.

8Conclusions

The language-model gate carries real, independently significant information about whether an after-hours earnings reaction will continue. It survives five years, six calendar years of regimes, both directions, and every liquidity quintile; it is uncorrelated with the market; and no leverage of a broad index reproduces its unbracketed path. The evidence available so far does not support the hypothesis that this is memorisation, though the two controls that would test it directly remain unrun.

The strategy as currently configured, however, does not capture that information. Its own stop-loss removes roughly three-quarters of the signal's value and leaves total return below a passive index. The immediate implication is not to trade this as specified but to re-derive the exit policy — the bracket width, and whether a hard stop belongs on a one-session horizon at all — and to re-run the identity ablation before treating the result as established.
