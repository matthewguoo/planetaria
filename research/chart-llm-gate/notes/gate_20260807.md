# Reading anonymised charts — 2026-08-07

Charts carry no ticker, no date and no price level; every bar is a percentage of the decision bar's close. There is no outcome-paired text for these windows in any corpus, so unlike the PEAD study there is nothing here for a model to recall. See `research_chart_render.py` for the full statement of that claim and its measured limit.

Costs 2bp round trip. `spread` = mean net bp on trades the gate KEPT minus mean net bp on trades it VETOED.

## `phi4` / `gate` — gate spread by strategy

The model reads the chart and calls it bullish or bearish; the gate keeps the trade when that agrees with the flagged side. Watch the `keeps` column before reading anything else — a keep rate near 100% means the model is agreeing with whatever it was handed, and the spread then has almost no vetoed trades under it.

`perm p` is p(spread >= observed) over 2000 shuffles of the verdict labels within (strategy, month). `tape acc` is how often the flagged side matched the sign of the move, which is a property of the SCREEN and is identical across arms.

### `phi4` — versus six numbers in a spreadsheet

A logistic regression on trailing return, realised vol, position in the day's range, relative volume, minutes since open and VWAP distance — fit walk-forward, never on the month it grades — thresholded to keep the same fraction of trades the model kept.

## Synthetic control — the same question asked of a random walk

Geometric Brownian motion at the universe's own volatility, rendered through the identical formatter. There is provably nothing to find. The accuracy is 50% by construction and is not the point; the CONVICTION DISTRIBUTION is. A model as confident on noise as on real tape is not deriving its confidence from the tape.
