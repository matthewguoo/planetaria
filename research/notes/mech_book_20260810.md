## What is honest about this and what is not

- **The diversification is real.** Four uncorrelated clocks (14:00–16:00
  decay, one auction minute, T+2 RTH, AH-entry overnight) at beta ≈ 0.03 is
  the robust finding — the stack, not any single sleeve, is where the book's
  Sharpe comes from. Solo, nothing here clears 2 except the delayed arm on
  its best window.
- **The 2024+ combined numbers are flattered by selection.** Every sleeve's
  edge was found on data that includes 2024-26, and 2024-26 is each sleeve's
  best stretch. The 2022+ view (Sharpe 1.55) is the humbler anchor, and it
  still contains gff's discovery window. None of this is forward-tested;
  the pre-registrations are the instrument for that.
- **New finding, fly:** the 15:30 close-out variant erases the edge
  (+0.03bp/day). The fly's P&L is the last 30 minutes of pin convergence —
  the registered 15:50 exit sits inside the value window it mostly
  forfeits. fly-1's exit choice deserves a measured revisit (vs assignment
  risk) before the live sample judges the wrong config.
- **New finding, 2026:** the mechanical PEAD arms (delayed ungated −3.6,
  gated −24.2, mech carry −64 net) are ALSO flat-to-negative in 2026 —
  the flagship's −30bp 2026 cell looks like drift regime, not LLM-gate
  failure. That partially answers the recall worry in the gate's favor:
  when the drift itself pays, the gate's selection paid on top of it.
- **Paper fills flatter** (the fleet note's own words) — gff most of all:
  its whole edge is one auction print.
